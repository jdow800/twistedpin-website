## TL;DR

**Singo (music bingo), Tuesday 7:00–9:00pm, launch late September 2026 (Sept 22 or 29), host-run.**

- Tuesday is the only night with zero conflict from anything — no competitor in Plainfield, no football, none of our own programming, no Q4 holiday-party demand.
- The new Tuesday leagues (start Sept 15) fix the "four people show up" risk: ~25–35 adults in the building 7–9:30 plus their non-bowling plus-ones, who are the seed audience.
- Singo is the best *hosted-game* fit for a bowling room specifically: noise-tolerant, low-attention, and the only format a group can play **while bowling** — i.e. the only one that can touch lane revenue.
- If Sunday is chosen instead: **5:30–7:30pm**, skip Bears late-window / SNF Sundays.

None of this is a jackpot. Success = ~40 people on a Tuesday by week six, a third of them on lanes. Commit 10–12 weeks; don't read the result before week six.

## 4. Format fit — Singo vs. alternatives for a bowling room

Two properties of the room decide this:
- **Noise.** Read-aloud trivia dies in a bowling alley. Music bingo is the one hosted game that's noise-tolerant (recognize a song in 4 seconds).
- **Attention.** Trivia = table-bound deliberation for 2 hrs. Singo = a glance every 90 sec → **a group can play while bowling.** Singo is the only format that can attach to lane revenue.

| Format | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Singo | Good | Noise-tolerant, low-attention, runs on lanes, broad 25–55 mixed-gender crowd (matches female-led-group thesis), low barrier, first-in-category, party-format bar spend. Host does all the work. |
| Karaoke | Good | Loud room, participatory, already doing it. Lower ceiling. |
| Trivia (generic) | Weak | Noise, attention, saturated (Craft'd Mon), narrow loyal low-spend crowd, never touches lanes. |
| Jeopardy! Bar League | Weak–OK | Best brand hook on the list; screen/phone formats survive noise better; still trivia. Only version worth considering: in the VIP suite with its own sound on a weeknight — a separate, smaller product. |
| Traditional bingo | Bad | Already ran the experiment. |
| Live music / comedy | Bad | Fights lanes for sound; comedy needs quiet more than trivia does. |
| Short-season social leagues | Better on paper | The format Bowlero/Pinstripes actually make money on, uses lanes by definition. **Jon: don't love them — fair effort for not much money.** Singo wins effort-adjusted because the host does the lifting. |

Effort-adjusted ranking: Singo > karaoke > trivia > social leagues > anything self-invented.

Sizing: mature Tone Singo night in this area ~30–60 players; good trivia night 20–40. Value = weeknight baseline that's zero today, a habit that feeds Friday, some lane hours on a dead night. Downside if it fails = a few hundred in prizes.

## 5. Promotion plan (ranked by impact)

**Tier 1 — decides whether it sticks**
1. **Prize design as retention.** Every prize redeemable *next visit* (lane credit, gift card, "free hour Tuesday"). **Progressive jackpot that rolls over** — the rollover number is the marketing ("Jackpot's at $340 this Tuesday"). Season leaderboard.
2. **Host's own audience.** Condition of hire: announced on `findmesingo` + their calendar, name-dropped at their other Plainfield games.
3. **Captive in-house audiences.** Mon/Tue league bowlers + plus-ones, Wed Penny A Pin, Thu karaoke: lane scoring screens between frames, bar table tents, bartender mention.

**Tier 2 — Jon's list, sharpened**
- **SMS — segment, don't blast.** NOT the FKB cohort (~2,500 parents of young kids — wrong audience, burns spring goodwill). Send to loyalty members w/ visit in last 90 days, and the Lane Rebook / checkout-opt-in guests (recent adult bowlers). One text week-before, one day-of. Rides existing consent rails.
- **Meta.** Event from venue page, host page co-hosts. Paid: 25–45, 10 mi, trivia/bar games/live music interests, $5–10/day first 3 Tuesdays only. Post the jackpot number weekly.
- **Google Business Profile Event post** + Event JSON-LD (reuse karaoke's `recurring` block pattern).
- **LED road sign** — rolling jackpot amount, not the event name.
- **Website** — `/upcoming-events` card, promo-bar slot for launch month, and a line on **TPRS confirmation emails + pay page** (highest-intent audience on the site, currently told nothing).
- **Roy** — one sentence max; callers ask about hours and parties.

**Tier 3 — cheap, real, smaller**
- Plainfield community FB groups / Nextdoor ("free, Tuesday, no football").
- Untappd venue event.
- Team framing — "bring your crew of 4–6."

**Skip:** print, radio, Yelp events, a grand-opening push (want week one fine, week six full — spread paid spend over the first three Tuesdays).
