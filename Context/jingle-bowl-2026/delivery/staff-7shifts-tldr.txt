JINGLE BOWL: THE PHONE-CALL BASICS

Sunday, Dec. 6 at Twisted Pin. Guests book and pay online at:
https://www.twistedpin.com/jingle-bowl/

Each reservation is 90 minutes. Starts: 1:45, 2:00, 2:30, 3:30 or 3:45pm, subject to availability. Booking closes 30 minutes before each start.

Traditional: $149.95 per lane, up to 5 guests.
VIP: $189.95 per lane, up to 6 guests.
Everyone attending counts, including non-bowlers. Add the 10% online booking fee and applicable tax; checkout shows the final total.

Both include bowling, shoes, one large pizza and one soda pitcher per lane (no refills), the cookie & cocoa bar, and a visit/photo with Santa on the guest's device. VIP also includes a basket of fries. Santa visits are coordinated by staff; he isn't bowling with each group.

Arrive on time, not early. Clear the lane and seating when bowling ends. Activities finish at 5:15pm; everyone must leave by 5:30pm.

No coupons or loyalty rewards. Cancel at least 72 hours beforehand to avoid forfeiting payment. Regular lane reservations aren't offered on Dec. 6.

For availability, send guests to the booking page. For special requests or policy exceptions, check with a manager before promising anything.
