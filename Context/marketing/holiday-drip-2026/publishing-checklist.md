# Before publishing or scheduling

Preparation does not authorize posting, scheduling, texts, emails or ad spend. Jon approves the selected creative and separately instructs the publishing action. Do not turn every optional variant into another send.

1. **Select the occasion.** Read `campaign.json` and the team's existing calendar. Check recent posts and sends with the owner/team; omit duplicates. Select one concept in each `creative_choice_group` used: batch 02 alternatives replace batch 01 visuals for the same occasion, without adding another post. Only entries with finalized assets/copy and recorded approval are eligible. The later calendar rows are concepts, not automatically ready posts.
   Follow the revised cadence: NYE October 28 / November 9 / November 17, opening November 18 at 9am, then varied follow-ups through December 28; Jingle weekly Fridays October 23 through December 4. November 17/18/20 is the intentional opening-week exception to usually one or two combined feed occasions. Avoid extra automatic reminders or a December 6 Jingle campaign post.
2. **Verify date and phase.** Use America/Chicago. October is UTC−05:00; from November 1 it is UTC−06:00. NYE opens November 18 at 9am Central, not December 31. Before that exact opening, use teaser copy only. On opening day, verify the public reservation entry is actually ready before releasing “open” copy; no purchase is needed. If a proposed publication date has passed, ask for a new date instead of silently scheduling it immediately.
3. **Read the art and caption together.** NYE absolute opening date/time and December 31 event date must remain clear. No party times/prices before opening. Jingle says December 6 and can invite bookings now. Never transplant NYE's opening countdown into Jingle. Recalculate any “days left” claim from the final publication timestamp; both prepared batches avoid this fragility.
4. **Use the individual full-size PNG.** 1080×1350 feed on Facebook/Instagram; 1080×1920 Story. Preview each selected placement, including grid/feed cropping and Story account/reply/link overlays. Reposition or use a separate composition if essential text is clipped. The contact sheet and QA images are for review only. Existing Facebook event covers are 1920×1005 and need the actual upload/reposition preview; a simulated centered 2.7:1 crop was not a real upload test.
5. **Verify the action.** Open the canonical event link on a phone. Facebook caption includes its direct URL. Instagram feed text saying “link in bio” requires a verified working bio/link-hub item for that event. A printed URL is not a clickable Story link; add the appropriate native link sticker where supported and verify it. Do not promise countdown/event notifications or organic reach. If a scheduler cannot add a necessary sticker, prepare a manual Story task rather than silently removing the action.
6. **Check claims.** “Our NYE parties usually sell out” is historical. No “selling fast,” “last few,” count of remaining lanes, or sold-out claim without a current event/party-specific inventory check by the owner/operator. Pause promotional booking calls after an event sells out or closes. Jingle package specifics, fees and Santa timing must match the current approved page; no Santa bowling/appearance-time promises. No coupons, signup rewards, $50 reward, invented age rules or guest-capacity marketing.
   Jingle is explicitly family-focused. Earlier NYE parties are very family friendly; later parties lean more adult while families also attend. No adults-only implication. NYE package copy may name bowling, shoes, one large pizza, one soda pitcher and party favors per lane; cocktails and other extras are separate. Do not turn the general "party essentials covered" angle into an unlimited-drinks or decor-service promise.
7. **Set Facebook event dates correctly.** NYE creation is planned for November 18 after opening; event bounds are Dec 31, 11am to Jan 1, 12:30am Central. Six separate reservations are explained in the existing approved event copy, not all-day admission. Going/Interested does not reserve a lane. Ask Jon for Jingle's actual event URL before using or creating a Facebook event; preparation files do not prove one exists.
8. **Keep ancillary channels selective.** Google teaser = Update; use current, appropriate event settings for an Event. Use only the selected SMS variant and the established consented marketing audience, after checking recent sends. Preserve the sending system's opt-out footer once, not twice; verify character encoding and final segment count including tracking/footer. No SMS is scheduled by this pack.
9. **Record the actual result.** After separately authorized action, save the real platform/account, post or schedule ID, local/UTC time and public URL (if available) in the manifest. “Prepared,” “approved,” “scheduled” and “published” are separate states. Partial success on Facebook does not prove Instagram succeeded. Never invent an ID or mark a failed action complete.
