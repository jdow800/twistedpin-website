# Holiday event drips: proposed calendar

Prepared October 22, 2026. **DRAFT / PROPOSED. Awaiting Jon's review. Nothing in this calendar is approved, scheduled, published or sent.** The first creative batch contains only the three concepts in [batch-01-copy.txt](batch-01-copy.txt).

This is an occasional addition to the social team's normal content. Start with about one NYE feed insertion a week before reservations open and one Jingle insertion every 10–14 days. Keep the combined event additions to **one or two feed concepts per week**, with lighter Stories near a meaningful date. Cross-posting one concept to Facebook and Instagram counts as one campaign insertion, not a reason to create two different announcements. If the team's calendar is already crowded, replace an event-related post or skip an optional row.

All proposed publishing times below are **America/Chicago**. These are practical planning choices, not measured best times for the account. October dates use CDT; November and December dates use CST. The fixed NYE opening is **Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 9am Central** (`2026-11-18T15:00:00Z`). All other publishing times can move with the social team's plan.

## Two events, two actions

- **NYE:** December 31. Before November 18 at 9am, ask people to save the reservations-opening date. The countdown is until booking opens. Keep party times and prices out of pre-opening creative. Jon confirms, “Our NYE parties usually sell out”; this describes history, not current inventory.
- **Jingle Bowl:** Sunday, December 6. Reservations are already open. Ask people to view packages and reserve. Use “Bowling, pizza & a visit with Santa.” Do not imply Santa bowls with guests or promise exact Santa appearance times.

Approved destinations:

- **NYE:** https://www.twistedpin.com/new-years-eve/
- **Jingle:** https://www.twistedpin.com/jingle-bowl/

Facebook captions use the full destination link. Instagram captions use a clearly labeled matching bio link only after the publisher verifies that link; otherwise use the plain website address in the alternate CTA provided. Add the actual destination as a Story link sticker. A printed URL on artwork is not a tappable link.

The baseline is a separate **1080 × 1350 (4:5) feed export for use on both Facebook and Instagram**, plus a **1080 × 1920 (9:16) Story composition**. A format is a delivery choice, not an extra publishing commitment. The existing wide Facebook event cover is for November 18 event creation. The social team can select a short motion treatment or real clip for later concepts; these dates are recommendations, not a requirement to reuse the same image each week.

## Working notes for the next batch

- The October 23 kickoff is optional. October 26 and 28 are the recommended first two insertions if the team wants the lightest start.
- Use absolute dates on time-sensitive art. This batch deliberately avoids numeric “days left” copy. If later work uses a number, calculate it from the actual publishing date/time; October 28, November 4 and November 11 at 9am are exactly three, two and one calendar weeks before opening in America/Chicago, across the DST change.
- On November 18, create the Facebook event for **December 31**, not the sales-opening date. Its overall duration spans the separate parties; Going/Interested does not reserve a lane. Use the existing post-opening source pack for the actual event settings and party schedule.
- Do not invent a Jingle Facebook event URL. The website is enough for this batch. Confirm the real event URL only if a later post is meant to link to it.
- Keep a short results note per published concept: publication URL, date/time, reach, shares/saves, link taps and attributable reservations where actually available. Likes alone do not show booking impact. Use these results and the team's normal calendar to choose the next batch.
