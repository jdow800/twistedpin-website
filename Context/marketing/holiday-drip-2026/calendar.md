The post-opening sequence rotates package essentials, earlier-family versus later-celebration choices, venue setting, group planning and the final week. Change the imagery/composition with the angle; do not simply repeat the mirrorball poster with new countdown numbers.

**Audience direction from Jon:** earlier NYE parties are very family friendly. Later parties lean more adult/friends/night-out, while families also attend. Reflect both audiences; never imply later parties are adults-only. The newer positioning report is a light creative reference, not a reason to suppress the explicit family angle.

**Package language:** "The party essentials are covered. You bring your people." Support this with bowling for the selected party time, shoes, one large pizza, one soda pitcher and party favors per lane. Cocktails, extra food/drinks and arcade cards are separate. Avoid a blanket all-inclusive or decor-service promise. A midnight toast belongs only to the 10pm party; keep that party time out of pre-opening copy.

After opening, use current availability before every booking invitation. Jon expects demand may remain bookable into late December, but that expectation is not an inventory check. "Our NYE parties usually sell out" is supported history; current "last few lanes" claims need fresh evidence. December 28 can become guest preparation if booking has closed or sold out.
