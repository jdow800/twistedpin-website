## What to carry through the series

Apply the report lightly: confident short lines, a little mischief, warm hospitality and a clear next action. One idea per piece; concrete inclusions over prestige adjectives. These events need not lead with cocktails.

Keep supplied logos, established fonts and recognizable Santa/Mirrorball direction. Use strong imagery and fewer words. Preserve the corrected NYE grip: two close finger holes and a distinctly lower thumb hole.

**Vary imagery by concept:** cinematic hero, real family bowling, actual food, Santa/photo or cocoa moments, and earlier/later celebrations. Use genuine approved assets to show the venue experience. Clearly illustrative fantasy is welcome; invented venue photos, guest scenes or amenities are not. Decorative gifts/ornaments do not promise included gifts or a decor service.
