Tonight needs a plan. Or the group chat needs someone to finally pick a
place. Here's our guide to a night out, a birthday, a team outing or a
slower afternoon in Plainfield, Illinois.

We're Twisted Pin, so the bar, bowling and party recommendations come
from our own venue. We've also included a local brewery and outdoor
options for a different kind of day.

## For things to do tonight

For karaoke or Singo music bingo, start with our
[upcoming-events calendar](/upcoming-events/). Pick a listed date and
check the time before rounding up the group; schedules can change around
holidays. On those nights, the music is very much the activity.

If you'd rather make your own evening, come in for drinks, something to
eat and a little competition. Check [today's hours and bowling
prices](/pricing/), then [reserve a lane](/reserve/) if bowling is part
of the plan. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours, subject to lane
availability.

## For a date night with cocktails and bowling

Start with the [craft cocktail menu](/menu/cocktails/), curated by Brian
Van Flandern, America's Top Mixologist (Food Network). Add something
from the [food menu](/menu/food/) to share. Bowling can settle who picks
the next date. Or who claims to have let the other person win.

Twisted Pin has [17 traditional lanes + a 6-lane VIP suite](/bowl/).
You can make bowling the plan or spend the evening at the bar. A lane
reservation isn't required to come in for food and drinks.

## For craft beer or an after-work drink

Our [28-tap self-serve beer and wine wall](/menu/taps/) lets you choose
your own pour and pay by the ounce. Try a small taste before committing
to a favorite. The selection rotates across craft beer, cider, wine and
other options; the live tap menu shows what's pouring.

If you'd like to visit a local brewery's own taproom,
[Werk Force Brewing](https://www.werkforcebrewing.com/locations) is on
South Center Street in Plainfield. Check its current taproom hours
before going. At Twisted Pin, the tap wall sits alongside cocktails,
food, bowling and the arcade, so a group with different tastes can
settle in together.

## For adult birthdays and company outings

The [six-lane semi-private VIP suite](/vip-suite/) is set apart from
the traditional lanes by a wall and accommodates up to 80 guests.
Full-venue buyouts accommodate up to 200. The event team confirms your
reserved space, party time, layout, catering and bar arrangements.

Choose the page that fits what you're planning:

- [Adult and milestone birthdays](/adult-birthday-parties/) for a 21st,
  30th, 40th or another birthday worth getting everyone together for.
- [Company events and staff appreciation outings](/corporate-events/)
  for team outings, employee celebrations and company parties.
- [Company holiday parties](/holiday-parties/) for Christmas parties
  and a year-end thank-you to the team.
- [Bridal showers, baby showers and gender reveals](/showers/).

Send your party plans through the inquiry form on those pages. Our
event team works through the details and arranges your booking.

## For bowling and arcade time with the kids

The traditional lanes have bumpers available for younger bowlers, and
the [arcade](/game/) gives everyone another round of competition after
the last frame. Food, bowling and games can stay in the same plan when
the weather changes.

Planning a child's birthday? Compare the [kids' birthday
packages](/birthday-parties/#kids), then [book a kids' party
online](/reserve/birthdays/). Adult birthdays use the event-planning
path above.

## For a walk or an afternoon outdoors

[Settlers' Park](https://www.plainfieldil.gov/community/settlers-park)
in historic downtown Plainfield has walking trails, a lake, a
playground and a StoryWalk. It's a good starting point for a walk,
a picnic or time outside with the kids. The Village's park page also
links its community calendar for seasonal concerts and movies.

Make that the afternoon, then choose dinner or drinks for the evening.
Check the park's current notices and event dates before planning around
a particular activity.

## For a school, church or team fundraiser

A [Twisted Pin bowling fundraiser](/fundraisers/) returns 50% of
eligible bowling, shoe and arcade sales to the host organization.
Supporters need to mention the fundraiser at check-in. The program
page explains how to arrange your night and what counts toward the
total.

Twisted Pin is in Plainfield, with free on-site parking. Coming from
Romeoville, Shorewood, Naperville, Joliet or Bolingbrook? Check our
[hours and directions](/pricing/) as you plan the trip.
